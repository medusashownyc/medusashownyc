About the show

Stilt Walkers & Robot

Roaming stilt performers and an interactive robot character who work through the event among your guests — creating visual presence from the first minute, no stage required.

It's the format that works best when you want the show to "be everywhere" — ideal for large activations, fairs and outdoor openings.
