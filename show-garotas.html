About the show

Garotas

Feather headdresses, carnival costuming and an energy that's contagious from the very first step. The garotas can work in roaming format — mixing in among your guests — or on a fixed stage.

It's one of the favorite formats for brand activations and themed parties: the burst of color is basically a guarantee of photos and social content.

30–45 min
1–6
Roaming or fixed
